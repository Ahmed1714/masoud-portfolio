I'm Masoud, a motion graphics designer obsessed with the space between stillness and movement — where a single frame becomes a world and timing becomes emotion. I bring brands, narratives, and ideas to life through purposeful animation and cinematic visual language.

With over seven years crafting motion for agencies, startups, and global brands, I've learned that the best animation isn't the flashiest — it's the one that makes the audience feel something they didn't expect.
